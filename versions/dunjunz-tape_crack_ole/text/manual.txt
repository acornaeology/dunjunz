                MANUAL for DUNJUNZ
                   by ------- E.S
                The Ranger uses a bow, and his quiver has
the power to produce an unlimited supply of arrows. He can
cast 2 fireball at a time, and each one does the same damage
as two arrows. A robe protects him and when hit he takes
maximum damage. Not a good fighter. He can fire 3 arrows ar
a time, but they are not that effective. He wears leather
armour, which absorbs aboyt 1/3 dammage, he does have one
advantage over the Barbarian and Warrior - he can use magic;
all monsters in the vicinity are killed, it drains his
energy badly.
The Wizaed has been taught how to cast poweful fireballs. He
can fire 3 arrows at a time, they are not that effective. He
wears leather armour, which absorbs about 1/3 damage, he
does have one advantage over the Barbarian and Warrior - he
can use magic; all monsters in the vicinity are killed, it
drains his energy badly.
The Barbarian's axe can creats clones of itself. She is a
born fighter with no magic. She begins with one axe which
does horrific damage and can kill in one shot ! Here natural
tough skin is better than any leather and nearly matches the
Warriors' chain mail!
The Warrior can draw an infinite amount of swords from her
sheath. She can throw 3 swords at a time, and each equals a
fireball in damage. She wears chainmail, carries a shield -
maximum armour! But, shee has no magic.
Each charactar can only fire a certain amount of weapons at
any one time; you need magic to maintain the weapon's
existence.

       OPJECTS.
*********************

 EXIT. To go deeper, walk over a square hole.

 TREASURE A square (med haandtag )

 FOOT. Increases health by 10 points (max 99). Worth 5 points

 TELEPORTER. A square with a square. Teleport on same level.

 TRAP DOORS. A Square with a door. Where new monsters emerge from.

 PILE OF WEAPONS. Alows a character to fire an extra weapon (max 3),
 worth 10 points.

 MAGIC HELMET. Increases armour raiting by 1 point. Less damage is
 taken when hit. Worth 10 points.

 CROSS OF RESURRECTION. Increases armour rauting by 1 point. Less
 damage is taken when hit. Worth 10 points.

 BOOTS OF SPEED. Moves you about twice the  normal speed. A lighting
 bolt on the display shows the presence of the boots. Bumping into
 anything apart from walls, doors and keys loses the boot. Worth 10
 points.

 ENERGY DRAINE. A square with 2 rektangel. Lose 20 health points.
 They can be destroyed by repeatedly shooting them. BEWARE when a
 weapon hits an energy drainer, it bounces back. Worth 1 point for
 every hit.

 MAGIC SWORD. Increases the damage a character's weapon does by 1.
 Worth 10 points.

 POTION OF HEALING. (Ligner en vase). A blessing or a curse. The
 health of the character changes: Take the last digit (the right
   most digit) of your score, duplicate it, and this value is the new
 health value. Worth 10 points.

 DOOR. Open by walking into it and by carrying the correct key, some
 are hidden. Worth 7 points.

 KEY. For each door there is a key. Only one key may be carried at a
 time . If you go over another key, then the key lying on the floor
 is picked up.
